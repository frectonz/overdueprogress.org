The Winners

3

Brief

This is Overdue Progress's first essay competition. We received 71 unique essays, covering everything from health to economics to agriculture and most things in between. I'm genuinely happy that this many people took part in our very first round, and I hope we'll see even more submissions in the competitions to come.

Evaluation happened in two stages. First, I read all 71 essays and took notes on each one, then used those notes to narrow the field down to a final 15. Those fifteen were distributed to judges, who scored them on the quality of their research, the originality of their thinking, the clarity of their writing, and the specificity and rigor of their analysis. From that, we turned the final 15 into a ranking, and the top 3 are the winners of this round.

Each of the three authors below wins 50,000 ETB. Congratulations to them, and thank you to everyone who wrote.
